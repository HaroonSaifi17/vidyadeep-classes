# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- **Primary Audience**: School students (Classes 6–12) preparing for Foundation, Board exams (Class 9–10, Class 11–12 Science/Commerce/Arts streams), and competitive exams (NEET, JEE Main & Advanced, CUET, NDA).
- **Secondary Audience**: Parents seeking reliable mentorship, regular attendance tracking, and transparent progress updates.
- **Career/Skill Learners**: Students and young adults seeking practical vocational and digital skills (Tally Prime + GST, Video Editing, Digital Marketing, Basic Computer, DTP & Design).

## Product Purpose
Vidyadeep Classes provides holistic academic coaching and practical skill training. The product (website) serves as an authoritative digital front door for prospective students and parents to evaluate programs, understand teaching methodology, explore exam prep tracks & skill lab options, and inquire/enroll.

## Positioning
A dual-tier learning institute combining rigorous academic coaching (small batch sizes capped at 24, concept-first teaching, daily practice, and weekly assessments) with real-world job skills (Skill Lab with Tally, Video Editing, Digital Marketing).

## Operating Context
- **Target Region/Context**: Local campus coaching center serving students preparing for school board examinations and national entrance tests.
- **Parent-Teacher Touchpoints**: Monthly progress reports, daily homework supervision, digital attendance, and open parent-teacher meetings.
- **Physical Facilities**: Smart air-conditioned classrooms, high-speed internet computer lab, library/study materials, CCTV surveillance.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Capped Batch Size**: Maximum 24 students per batch for personalized attention.
- **Academic Programs**:
  - Class 6–8: Foundation years (Maths, Science, English, SST, Hindi, Computer).
  - Class 9–10: Board preparation (Sample papers, answer writing, pre-boards).
  - Class 11–12: Specialist stream faculty for Science, Commerce, and Arts.
- **Competitive Exam Tracks**: NEET, JEE Main + Advanced, CUET, NDA.
- **Skill Lab Courses**: Basic Computer, DTP & Graphic Design, Tally Prime with GST, IT & Networking, Video Editing, Digital Marketing, E-Commerce.
- **Technical Stack**: React 19, TypeScript, Vite 8, Tailwind CSS v4, Oxlint.

## Brand Commitments
- **Name**: Vidyadeep Classes
- **Tone & Voice**: Academic yet approachable, transparent, grounded, encouraging, and focused on genuine understanding over rote memorization.
- **Established Assets**: Comprehensive structured content defined in `src/content/site.ts`.

## Evidence on Hand
- 7+ years of mentorship experience behind the campus.
- Structured curriculum & exam tracks defined in `src/content/site.ts`.
- 4 competitive exam tracks with dedicated test series & PYQ analysis.

## Product Principles
1. **Concept Over Rote**: Every lesson emphasizes fundamental understanding and daily problem-solving over memorization.
2. **Transparent Accountability**: Regular, honest communication with parents regarding student attendance, test performance, and growth areas.
3. **Dual Academic & Practical Value**: Empowering students both academically for boards/entrance exams and practically for modern career skills.
4. **Capped & Focused**: Maintaining small class sizes (max 24) to ensure no student gets left behind.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast typography for scannability by parents and students alike.
- Clear structural hierarchy, accessible touch targets on mobile, and semantic HTML structure.
